# Veritris Oklahoma Electric Power Company

### Powering Texoma. Connecting Tomorrow.
![alt text](https://github.com/veritris-limited/veritris/blob/released/assets/veritris-axiom.png?raw=true) 

**Veritris Oklahoma Electric Power Company** is a strategic energy initiative of Veritris focused on building a software-defined energy, commercial power, wholesale electricity, and energy-infrastructure platform serving Oklahoma, the Texoma region, and ultimately the broader South-Central United States.

This repository — **the-fista** — serves as a development, research, architecture, modeling, and documentation environment for the Veritris Energy platform and the Oklahoma Electric Power Company initiative.

> **Status:** Development / Strategic Planning  
> **Primary Market:** Oklahoma and Texoma  
> **Expansion Markets:** Texas, Kansas, Arkansas, Missouri  
> **Industry:** Energy Technology, Electric Power, SaaS, Wholesale Energy, Infrastructure  
> **Parent Platform:** Veritris

---

## Mission

Veritris Energy's mission is to build a modern multi-utility platform that connects customers, businesses, energy assets, utilities, telecommunications infrastructure, and wholesale electricity markets through a unified digital operating environment.

Rather than attempting to immediately duplicate the poles, substations, transmission lines, and generation fleets of incumbent electric utilities, Veritris is developing a technology-first model designed to operate **above and alongside existing energy infrastructure**.

The long-term vision is:

**Energy Software → Energy Services → Utility Partnerships → Power Marketing → Wholesale Energy → Regional Energy Infrastructure**

---

## The Texoma Strategy

Veritris Energy is designed around the economic relationship between **Oklahoma and North Texas**, creating a natural operating corridor across the Texoma region.

The company's regional strategy is intended to combine:

- Electric energy services
- Wholesale electricity procurement
- Commercial and industrial energy management
- Data-center and large-load energy solutions
- Distributed energy resources
- Battery and storage management
- Solar and renewable-energy integration
- Demand-response technology
- Fiber-optic telecommunications
- 5G / fixed wireless connectivity
- Cloud and managed infrastructure
- AI-driven energy analytics

This creates a broader infrastructure model in which Veritris can manage both the **digital network and energy relationship** of a customer.
